# Brownian-motion-near-soft-interfaces

Experimental data and analysis codes related to the dynamics of confined soft colloids (oil microdroplets) diffusing in water near a glass wall. For more details, see:

Fares, et al. "Observation of Brownian elastohydrodynamic forces acting on confined soft colloids." Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 121.42 (2024): e2411956121.
